# Published HGP classical seeds

Numerical support lists transcribed from the ancillary data of Freire, Delfosse,
and Leverrier, https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09622v3 (ISIT 2025).
The first line is the shape of the classical check matrix; remaining lines are
zero-based supports of its rows. These are 15-by-20 **classical seeds**, not the
625-qubit quantum check matrices. Taking HGP(H,H) gives [[625,25]].

PEG is a progressive-edge-growth reference and SA was optimized for a quantum
**erasure** objective. This does not establish superiority under circuit noise.
The supplied matrices are not assumed to be exactly (3,4)-regular.
Original file URLs and hashes of the transcribed numerical records are recorded
in manifest.json. Distances are computed from the small classical kernel and the
full-row-rank HGP distance relation, rather than taken from a filename.
